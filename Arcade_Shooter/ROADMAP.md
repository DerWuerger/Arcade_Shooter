# Roadmap

## Completed

### 0.0.1 - Rail Foundation

- `AArcadeRailPath` with editable spline foundation
- `AArcadeRailMover` with minimal non-looping movement along an assigned rail path

## Next

Future versions can build on the rail foundation with gameplay systems such as camera behavior, input, combat stops, triggers, enemies, weapons, and shooting.

No future gameplay scope is implemented in version 0.0.1.
